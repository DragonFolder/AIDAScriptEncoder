How a sigil is made

Each letter is a line between two of eight points on a circle (its two semaphore directions). Long words split into overlapping circles, and the words of a sentence join into one figure. ‹ Layout › tries other ways of putting it together.

The dot marks the first letter, the crossbar the last. Downloads don't contain your sentence.
